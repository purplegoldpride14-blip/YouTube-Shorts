You're standing at the edge of a decision you can't take back, heart pounding, palms sweating, waiting for a sign that never comes. Fear doesn't ask permission. It walks in and starts talking, telling you everything that could go wrong before you've even tried. But faith doesn't wait for certainty. Faith moves while the outcome is still unknown, one unsteady step at a time. You don't need proof it'll work out. You need enough courage for today. The moment you stop demanding answers and start trusting the One who already has them, fear loses its grip. Fear ends where faith begins.
